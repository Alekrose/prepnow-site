Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Who is responsible

PrepNow (CVR 46801822) is the data controller for the information processed about you. Write to kontakt@prepnow.dk with any questions.

What stays on your phone

Your reminders. Everything else you enter is also stored on our server (see below), so it can follow you to a new phone.

A MobilePay number in Madklub is stored only on the phone and never sent to us.

You can delete your history under Goals. Deleting the app removes everything on the phone. The copy on our server is deleted when you delete the account under My account → Delete account. If you are not signed in, it is also deleted by itself after 180 days without use (see How long).

Allergies and diet

Exclusions such as lactose, gluten, nuts or shellfish may count as health data. We only use them to filter out dishes, and only because you chose them (your consent). You can remove them at any time under Profile.

What we store on our server

The app gets a random user ID the first time you open it, also without an account. Under that ID, your settings (the name you entered, the household with names and goals per person, your goals and setup answers, exclusions, stores, regular items, purchases on the list, ratings, favourites, weekly check-in answers and plan history) and how you use the app (for example which dishes you open, swap and rate, tied to the user ID) are stored with our database provider on servers in the EU (Frankfurt). We use this to keep a copy of your settings and to make your meal plans better. Combined across all users and without names, ratings and favourites decide which known dishes come back in the coming weeks. The legal basis is our agreement with you to provide the app. For usage statistics, the basis is our legitimate interest (see Statistics and reminders).

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we also store your e-mail or the ID they give us, so your data can follow you to a new phone.

If the app asks how PrepNow is tasting so far, your answer (really good or could be better, what you pick and write, the app version and your phone’s operating system) is stored under your user id on our servers in the EU so we can improve the app. Only we can read it, and it is deleted with your account. Your answer makes no difference to what you get in the app.

If you say a dish was not for you, you can pick a reason and write a short comment. It is stored under your user ID with your ratings at our database provider in the EU, so we can fix recipes and pick better dishes, and it is deleted with your account.

If you delete your account under My account → Delete account, everything on the server and on the phone is deleted at once. From our backups it is gone within 30 days at the latest.

Student price

If you choose the student price, we send a code to your student e-mail through our servers in the EU. Once you enter it, we store the address on your account with when it was verified, so we can give you the price and verify it again after 12 months. The e-mail stays on the account until you delete the account.

Family

If you are in a family, we store on our servers in the EU who is in it, the name you entered in the app, and your shared document: settings, this week's plan, swapped dishes, the shopping list and what has been bought, your own items and snacks. Everyone in the family can see it.

To make sure the family lives under one roof, we store a one-way fingerprint (a salted hash) of your home network's public internet address, and when each phone was last on it. The address itself is not stored, and we cannot see where you live. The legal basis is our agreement with you to provide Family.

If you leave the family, or it ends, your membership is deleted at once; if the family ends, the shared document and the fingerprint are deleted too.

Payment

Subscriptions are bought and paid through Apple or Google. We never see your card details. To keep track of subscriptions we use a subscription provider in the USA. It receives your random user ID, your purchases and subscription status from Apple or Google, and app, phone and country details, and processes them on our behalf. The transfer to the USA is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses. If you delete your account, we also delete you at the subscription provider.

Under your user ID we store on our servers in the EU which subscription you have and when it ends, plus a short log of its events (for example purchase, renewal and cancellation: which subscription, whether it is a trial, which store, when, and for a cancellation the reason the store gives, such as that you cancelled yourself or that a payment failed). We use it to give you what you paid for. We do not store the price, your country or receipt numbers. It is deleted with your account.

If you cancel Pro, we ask why. Your answer (the reason, anything you write, and whether you stayed or stopped) is stored under your user id on our servers in the EU so we can improve the app. Only we can read it, and it is deleted with your account.

AI and meal plans

Meal plans are made by an AI model based on the week's deals. The weekly plan is made once for all users and contains no information about you; we send nothing about you to the AI provider. Your own week is picked by the app from the finished dishes, based on your answers.

Technical information

The app gets its updates from Expo (650 Industries Inc., USA). Each time the app checks for an update, Expo receives your IP address, phone and system type, the app version and an install ID. Expo uses this on our behalf to send the right update. The transfer to the USA is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses.

Product photos from the flyers load straight from the offer sites' image servers. They see your IP address and phone type when a photo loads, but we send them nothing about you.

Our database provider sees your IP address and phone type when the app saves or loads data, and uses them to keep your sign-in secure.

Statistics and reminders

There are no ads in PrepNow, and we do not use an advertising ID.

Usage statistics (for example whether you open deals, Madklub or Pro) and error reports (a short error message when the app crashes) are tied to your random user ID, and to your account if you are signed in. We do not put your name or e-mail in them, and they stay with us: we use no advertising or analytics companies. We use them to fix bugs and improve the app, based on our legitimate interest in doing so.

You can turn this off under Settings → Share usage statistics. The app then no longer stores or sends them. What you do with dishes (opening, cooking, rating, saving as a favourite and swapping), what you tick off on the list, your goals and your weekly check-in answers are still sent, because your meal plans are built from them.

Reminders are off until you turn them on under Settings.

How long

Information on the phone stays there until you delete it or the app. Account data is kept while you use the app. If you delete the account under My account, it is deleted at once, and from our backups it is gone within 30 days at the latest. If you use the app without signing in and it has not been opened for 180 days, we delete the account and everything that belongs to it ourselves (not while you have an active subscription).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your information, to restrict processing, to receive your data and to object. Consent can always be withdrawn. You can download your data yourself under My account → Download my data and delete it under Delete account. Write to us about anything else and we will reply within one month.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, datatilsynet.dk.

Children

PrepNow is not directed at children under 16. If we learn that a child under 16 uses the app, we delete the child's account and data.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
