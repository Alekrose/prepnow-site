The service

PrepNow makes meal plans and shopping lists from supermarket flyers. The app is run by Thomas Rosenberg (CVR 46801822). The free version is free. Pro is a subscription with extra features.

Prices and deals

Prices come from public flyers and may be wrong, sold out or changed. The store's price at the till always applies. We do not sell food and are not affiliated with Netto, REMA 1000, Lidl, 365discount, Føtex, Bilka, SuperBrugsen, Kvickly or any other store.

Nutrition and allergies

Nutrition values are estimates calculated from the Danish Food Composition Database (DTU National Food Institute) and may differ from the actual products. PrepNow is not medical advice.

If you have an allergy, always read the product label. If you are ill, pregnant, have an eating disorder or follow a special diet for medical reasons, talk to your doctor or a dietitian before following a plan.

Subscription and trial

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. After that the subscription renews automatically at the price shown until you cancel.

Cancel in the App Store or Google Play at least 24 hours before the next renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules.

Student price

The student price requires a valid e-mail from a Danish educational institution and is verified again after 12 months. If it cannot be verified, the student subscription ends at the end of its period, and you can choose Pro instead. You are told well in advance.

Family

Family is for up to 4 people who live in the same household. The person who pays creates the family at home, and the others join on the home's Wi-Fi. Every phone must be on the home network at least once every 31 days; otherwise it is paused until it is home again.

The person who pays can remove members and move the family to a new home network. If the subscription is cancelled, the family ends for everyone. We may close families that clearly do not live together.

Using the app

You may use the app for yourself and your household. You may not copy plans or data at scale, resell them or try to get around payment.

Liability

We work to keep plans, prices and nutrition values correct but cannot guarantee them. We are not liable for losses caused by wrong prices, sold-out items or cooking from the app's recipes, beyond what Danish law requires.

Law and complaints

These terms follow Danish law. Write to kontakt@prepnow.dk if you are unhappy. You can also complain to the Danish Consumer Complaints Board through naevneneshus.dk.
